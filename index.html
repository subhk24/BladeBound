HOW TO PLAY

🎯 GOAL

Survive endless waves of enemies in the arena using your 5 Lives. Defeat enemies to score points and set your personal best!

🎮 CONTROLS
A / D or ◀ ▶ Move Left / Right
J or Space Attack (Combo Flurry)
K or Shift Dodge Roll (Invulnerable)
P or ESC Pause / Quit Menu
⚔️ 3-PHASE COMBO SYSTEM

Tap Attack consecutively to unleash your full hero combination:

  • Phase 1 (Hits 1-2): Quick forward slash (15 DMG)
  • Phase 2 (Hits 3-4): Forward dash lunge burst (25 DMG)
  • Phase 3 (Hits 5-6): Overhead jump to downward ground slam (40 DMG)
❤️ 5 LIVES & ⚡ STAMINA

You start with 5 hearts. When your HP reaches 0, you lose 1 life and respawn with 1 second of shield invulnerability. Attacking costs blue Energy, which recharges automatically when resting.
